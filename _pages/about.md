I am a first-year <a href='https://mrsd.ri.cmu.edu/'>MRSD</a> student in the <a href='https://www.ri.cmu.edu/'>Robotics Institute</a> (RI) at <a href='https://www.cmu.edu/'>Carnegie Mellon University</a>. My long-term goal is to push the frontier of physical intelligence through **autonomous skill discovery**, enabling robots to learn from their own experience rather than being limited to what humans demonstrate. I am currently focusing on **Agentic Robotics** and **Video-based Learning** (e.g., my capstone on <a href='{{ "/projects/MRSD_capstone/" | relative_url }}'>learning humanoid manufacturing tasks from human video</a>).

I have 2.5 years of industry experience spanning robotic manipulation (UMI/Diffusion Policy), quadruped robotics (vision-language navigation, learned locomotion policy selection, learning-based control), 3D reconstruction (3DGS), sensor fusion, and autonomous mobile robots for warehouse logistics.

Previously, I obtained my B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering from <a href='https://www.ntu.edu.tw/english/'>National Taiwan University</a>.
